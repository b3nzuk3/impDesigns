# Website expansion surface brief
Mode: Persuade. Approved extension of the existing industrial identity, not a redesign. Kenyan business visitors compare real work and prepare a WhatsApp enquiry.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Broaden the offer without pretending unapproved catalogue pages are ready. Real tape/tag work remains the proof.
OWN-WORLD: Existing warm #fbfaf7 ground, charcoal #111111, orange #ea580c, bold Inter headings, squared controls, dark borders and existing offset shadows.
STORY: Understand the expanded business, choose a family, inspect supported products and real work, prepare an enquiry and explicitly send it in WhatsApp.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Preserve photographic carousel and left-aligned white headline; replace packaging-only headline with printing, branding and signage. Primary quote action stays below the introduction. Six family entry points follow, distinguishing catalogue pages from enquire-only families.
FORM: User-pinned existing surface extension; no concept seed required. Desktop dropdown and mobile collapsible family list use native disclosure semantics.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

No new raster assets planned. Existing supplied artwork, real R2 photography and portfolio attribution retained. Only two offerings published; fourteen draft. No empty category pages.
